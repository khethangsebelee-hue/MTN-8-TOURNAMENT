About People's Cup

The MTN 8 Cup Tournament is one of the biggest football competitions in South Africa. The top eight football clubs compete for trophies, prizes, and football glory.

Football fans enjoy exciting matches, entertainment, and unforgettable moments during the tournament. The event promotes teamwork, sportsmanship, and talent development.
